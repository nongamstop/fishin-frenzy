Our editorial approach

Frenzy Game is designed to be direct, independent and practical. We avoid unnecessary hype and do not describe gambling products as “safe”, “guaranteed” or “risk-free”. When a topic involves uncertainty, we explain that uncertainty rather than hiding it.


Our editorial standards include:


  • using clear language instead of promotional exaggeration;
  • separating factual information from opinion;
  • checking important claims before publication;
  • highlighting restrictions, wagering rules and player risks;
  • updating pages when material information changes;
  • including responsible gambling guidance where relevant.

If we discuss bonuses, games or casinos, we aim to explain the conditions as well as the headline offer. A large bonus is not automatically good value, and a high RTP does not mean a player will win.
